# Marketing intel

Signals from LinkedIn, competitors and the market, evaluated for Ranger marketing.

Rules
- One entry per signal, newest first.
- Every claim carries a source and a status: verified, secondary, or unverified.
- Third-party figures are context only. We never present them as Ranger results.
- Old entries are marked superseded, never deleted.

Status key
- **Verified**: checked against the primary source.
- **Secondary**: confirmed by a summary or transcript service, not the primary source.
- **Unverified**: only seen in the post that surfaced it.

## 2026-11-05. Palantir: "The atomic unit is the workflow"

**Source**: LinkedIn post shared by Yana, on Palantir's Q3 2026 earnings call (Monday 2 Nov 2026).
**Verdict**: use it. Strong fit with how we already sell.

### What was said

1. **Workflow, not worker.** Shyam Sankar, Palantir CTO: the enterprise unit is the workflow, not the knowledge worker. Productivity gains alone can show no ROI. Software development is the exception, because there the knowledge worker is the workflow. Status: secondary ([Yahoo Finance transcript](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/PLTR/earnings/PLTR-Q3-2026-earnings_call-722806.html), [BigGo summary](https://finance.biggo.com/quote/PLTR/earnings-call/US_PLTR_2026-11-02), [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/transcript-what-palantir-technologies-pltr-said-on-its-q3-earnings-call-2026-11-02/)). Exact wording of the quotes in the post not confirmed.
2. **New deal shape.** Alex Karp, CEO: buyers now arrive saying what they have does not work and their IP is at risk. Faster to deployment, shorter first contract, then large expansion. Status: unverified.
3. **Expansion examples.** Home appliance company: about $1M pilot in Q2 to $15M a year in Q3, across commercial, finance and supply chain. Industrial materials company: nearly $3M pilot in Q2 to $29M a year in Q3. Status: unverified.
4. **Revenue mix.** Post says roughly 50/50 government and commercial. Q3 2026 revenue $2.313B, US commercial $984M. Status: verified for the totals ([8-K press release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001321655/000132165526000045/a2026q3ex991pressrelease.htm)). The 50/50 split is unverified.

### Why it matters for Ranger

1. **Same unit of value.** We already sell workflows: inquiry to order, order to remittance, tendering. A large, credible voice now says the market should buy this way. Borrowed authority for our framing.
2. **Same deal motion.** Short paid PoC on one workflow, expansion only if it works. This is our PoC scoping and commercial packaging stage. The Palantir examples show buyers accept a small first contract with a large second one.
3. **Same measurement.** "Stop counting logins, measure what the workflow produces" is our value evidence stage and RAM in one line. Quote turnaround, inquiries processed, hit rate, not seats or adoption.
4. **Industrial buyers named.** One of the two expansion examples is an industrial materials company. That is our buyer.

### How we could use it

1. **Thought leadership.** A post from Sari: the workflow is the unit, applied to industrial techno-commercial work. Cite the call, add our view on ITO.
2. **Discovery and PoC framing.** One line for decks and first calls: "We start with one workflow and measure what comes out of it."
3. **Objection handling.** When a buyer asks for a broad copilot rollout or seat pricing, this gives us a neutral, cited reason to narrow to one workflow.

### Cautions

1. **Quote the call, not the post.** Confirm the exact wording and the deal figures in a primary transcript before any external use.
2. **No comparison.** Palantir's deal sizes are not our benchmark. Use them to show the motion, never as expected Ranger numbers.
3. **Scale gap.** Palantir sends forward deployed engineers. Worth deciding how we describe our own delivery model so the parallel does not overpromise.
