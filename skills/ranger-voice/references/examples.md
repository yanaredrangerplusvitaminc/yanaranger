# Examples: the voice at its best

Real messages sent by Sari and Yana between September and November 2026, lightly cleaned (missing full stops restored, signatures removed). Use them for rhythm and register, not as templates to paste. Two prospects should never receive the same sentence.

## 1. Executive session request, senior GCC (Yana, after Sari's edits, Emdad, 16 Oct)

> Dear Mr. Mohamed and Mr. Atul,
>
> Thank you for your discussion with Sari yesterday.
>
> As a next step, we would like to propose a 90-minute session at your office with your business unit heads, led by Sari Saadi, along with our AI Solutions Director, Ibraheem Barbahan.
>
> In this session we will map, at a high level, your organization's techno-commercial flows and identify where AI can create the most value. We will then return to you with a first roadmap of priorities, timeline and investment phases.
>
> Would one of these times suit you?
>
> • Wednesday 21 October, 9.30 or 15.00
> • Friday 23 October, 10.00
>
> If another time works better, we will gladly adjust. Please feel free to include any colleagues you would like to join.
>
> Warm regards,

Why it works: opens on the real conversation, names the team by role with Sari first, says what the session does in their organization, gives options and an easy out.

## 8. Same message, approachable register (Yana, Inception42, Nov; rated 95% with version 7)

Use for peers and partners who are senior but relaxed. Still royal warmth and C-level, just simpler and more easygoing.

> Hi Carmen,
>
> I hope you're well.
>
> Sari really enjoyed meeting you and Islam at AI Everything. Our work with industrial companies on their tenders and bids keeps growing, and he thought Inception42 could fit naturally alongside it.

Version 7 is the high-profile register (formal salutation, full forms, slower pace). Version 8 is approachable (Hi, contractions, shorter sentences). Both keep the host posture and one open door.

## Sari's edits to drafts (what he changes)

| Draft | Sari's version | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| "Thank you for today, and for thinking of us for Khadamat." | "Thank you for your time today, and we look forward to a discovery session with Khadamat." | Turn thanks into a named next step. |
| Generic line about industrial clients | "A security or cleaning contract goes through much of the same bid and handover work our industrial clients deal with." | Name their own services so they see the fit. |
| "we work with a few pump companies on pump bidding" | "we work with global EPCs and OEMs, including well-established international pump companies" | No hedges, no narrow labels, no competitor cues. |
| Apology email about a product issue | "This type of message emphasizes the issue and not the opportunity." | Lead with what happens next for them. |
| "for making it possible for me to attend" | "for the kind invitation" | Sari is the invited guest, never the beneficiary of a favour. |
| "meet our co-founder" | "our director" / "Head of Partnerships" | Credibility without looking small. |

## Sari's line for a non-pushy next step (1 Nov)

"I came here to build a relationship with you, and based on the alignment and shared interest here, it's clear that we will keep talking, and I look forward to that." Then: "When is a good time for the next conversation? I can bring our director to meet you during the show, or schedule something for the second week after. Which should we do?" Give them the choice and hold yourself to it.

## Do not copy (real slips)

- Typos: "availabilties suite", "Ai Everything", "look forward to meet you", "I dont".
- "we look forward to keeping the momentum going" (implies they are stalling).
- "one of the most advanced industrial tendering platforms globally", "a service unlike any other".
- "while we are US-based..." (defensive).
- The same template sentence sent to two prospects on the same day.
- Six use cases in one sentence. Name one, two at most.
