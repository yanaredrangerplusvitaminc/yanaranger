## Conflicts settled in this skill (confirm with Yana and Sari)

1. **Sari's title.** Confirmed by Yana, 3 Nov: "Head of Partnerships". Never "co-founder" in emails or messages; it signals small.
2. **Category term.** "Techno-Commercial AI" as the category name, lowercase "techno-commercial" in running text, always paired with something concrete. Working term, not final (Sari, 23 Oct).
3. **"I hope you are well."** Confirmed by Yana, 3 Nov: fine. Never "I hope this email finds you well".
4. **"I wanted to follow up".** Banned, including in Sari's drafts (rule post-dates his 13 Oct emails).
5. **Bullets.** Prose in prospect emails; bullets allowed for time slots and agendas with senior contacts.
6. **Warm words.** "glad to" and "happy to adjust" are fine; avoid "love", "thrilled", "pleased to connect" with people we have not met.
7. **Contractions.** First formal contact in the Gulf: none. Warm threads, Western peers, LinkedIn and WhatsApp: fine.
8. **Numbers.** None in GCC outreach unless sourced and approved. North America LinkedIn stats stay in North America.
9. **Who Yana writes as.** Herself, as a peer. Not "Sari asked me to follow up".
10. **"That's on us".** Sari keeps it deliberately as ownership language; fine when we own a delay or a fix, not as filler.
11. **Inquiry to cash.** Avoid in outreach; say what clients use today (inquiry to order) and keep order to remittance as direction.
12. **Location line.** Do not write "regional office in Abu Dhabi" or "Abu Dhabi and Vancouver" until confirmed.
13. **Yana's title.** Confirmed by Yana, 3 Nov: "Director of Client Experience". In the signature only.
