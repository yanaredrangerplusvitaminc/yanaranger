# Voice experiment: a composite of seven brands

Status: experiment, 27 Oct 2026. Not approved. Yana asked for a combination of Adobe, Claude, ChatGPT, Apple, Salesforce, SAP and Slack editorial voices (Slack added after Sari named it, 27 Oct), to test as a model for Ranger.

## What each brand says about its own voice

| Brand | Their stated voice | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Adobe | **Rational** (research-informed; not overly opinionated, funny or trendy), **Human** (friendly, honest, responsible; varied sentences), **Focused** (concise; describe only what is needed; do not invent new concepts or names). Voice stays; tone slides with the situation. | Spectrum, Voice and tone: https://spectrum.adobe.com/page/voice-and-tone |
| Salesforce | **Honest, Clear, Fun, Inspiring.** Voice is the stable personality; tone adjusts to the audience (developers want directness, salespeople want brief scannable text). | SLDS Voice and tone: https://spring-21.lightningdesignsystem.com/guidelines/voice-and-tone |
| SAP | **The approachable expert**: technical expertise balanced with empathy and respect. Traits: clear, informative, approachable, optimistic. BTP version: CIAO (clear, insightful, approachable, optimistic). "We" refers to SAP or the software, used carefully. | Fiori UX writing basics: https://www.sap.com/design-system/fiori-design-web/v1-148/foundations/writing-and-wording/ux-writing/ux-writing-guidelines/basics ; BTP tone of voice: https://www.sap.com/design-system/btp/writing-guidelines/basics/basics-of-ua/tone-of-voice |
| Apple | The Apple Style Guide keeps one consistent voice across documentation, training and interfaces. Observed in Apple marketing [not from the guide]: short declarative lines, the product as the subject, few adjectives, one idea per line. | Apple Style Guide: https://support.apple.com/guide/applestyleguide/welcome/web |
| Slack | **Clear, concise, human**: "We are humans, speaking to humans." Speaks to the reader directly, invites conversation, rewards the curious. Copy principles include "Don't make me think" and "Make it memorable". Character with restraint: confident but not cocky, witty but not silly, friendly but not ingratiating; character never overwhelms content. Tone varies by audience and context. | Slack design, The voice of the brand: https://slack.design/articles/thevoiceofthebrand-5principles/ |
| Claude (Anthropic) | [Not sourced; search stopped.] Observed: plain, warm, direct; explains without hype; admits limits. | Observation only |
| ChatGPT (OpenAI) | [Not sourced; search stopped.] Observed: simple, friendly, outcome-first; short sentences; everyday words. | Observation only |

## The composite: what we keep, what we leave

Keep:
1. **The approachable expert** (SAP). We know the industry deeply and we speak to people as equals.
2. **Rational, human, focused** (Adobe). No trendiness, no jokes, no invented terms; varied sentences; only what is needed.
3. **Honest and clear** (Salesforce). Voice fixed, tone adjusted to the reader: a CEO gets the business result, an engineer gets the specifics.
4. **Restraint** (Apple, observed). Short lines. The subject does the work. Few adjectives.
5. **Plain warmth** (Claude and ChatGPT, observed). Everyday words, no hype, honest about limits.
6. **Life and colour with restraint** (Slack). Humans speaking to humans; reward the curious reader; confident not cocky, witty not silly, friendly not ingratiating; character never overwhelms content. This is the 30% modern innovator in Sari's enterprise posture.

Leave:
- Salesforce's "fun" and Slack's playfulness beyond a light touch. Serious is never loud.
- Apple's product-as-hero. Our client is the hero.
- Optimism that promises outcomes. SAP's optimism becomes our confidence, backed by proof.

## Ranger in one line (experiment)

**The approachable industrial expert: rational, human and focused; honest and clear; restrained in form; warm in plain words, with a touch of Slack's life.**

## Test: the OTC post in the composite voice

> In May, Kyle Jordan and Sari Saadi were at OTC in Houston.
>
> They met leaders across offshore and energy, then spent time with our clients there, beside the equipment they engineer and bid. Every offer starts with that equipment: its specs set the price.
>
> That link is our work. For clients around the world, we connect the technical and commercial sides of a project, from inquiry all the way to cash.
>
> Thank you to everyone who gave us their time, and to our hosts in Houston.

Notes on the test: short opening line (Apple), one concrete fact as proof (Adobe focused), plain explanation of why it matters (SAP approachable expert), no adjectives doing the work (Salesforce honest), warm close without a pitch (Claude).
