## Genuine founder email examples
Source: Sari's Ali & Sons thread in Yana's connected Gmail, 14 September to 13 October 2026.

- 'Thank you Mr. Rabee, Dear Shumon,'
- 'We look forward to scheduling a brief call with you this week (or next) to introduce our AI-as-a-Service solution for techno-commercial workflows.'
- 'Could we schedule a meeting for this Thursday or Friday, or sometime next week based on your availability? I am also happy to meet at your office if that works best for you.'

Learn from the rhythm, specificity and relational warmth rather than copying those phrases every time.

## Core positioning
Source: Ranger BE Sari Voice & Messaging Playbook, 7 October 2026, and Sari Saadi TOV notes, 28 September 2026.

- Treat Ranger as a trusted AI partner that stays accountable for industrial outcomes across inquiry-to-cash.
- A client introduction carries the introducer's reputation; show real delivery before asking for introductions.
- Use proof carefully: confirmed business outcomes or an approved customer case, never speculative metrics.
- First win, then next stage; avoid selling the full platform in an initial follow-up.
