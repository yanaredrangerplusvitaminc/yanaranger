# Ranger editorial system: brand voice guidelines

## First principle: "Пиши, сокращай" (Write, Cut)

The book by Maxim Ilyakhov and Lyudmila Sarycheva is the bible of everything we write. (Yana, 27 Oct)

- **Every sentence must mean something.** If a line could be deleted and the reader would lose nothing, delete it. "Comparing notes", "we look forward to…", "exciting times", "in today's fast-moving world" mean nothing. We never publish things that mean nothing.
- **Facts over evaluations.** Replace adjectives and claims ("leading", "innovative", "great") with the fact that would make the reader conclude it themselves.
- **Cut stop words:** filler, empty intensifiers, hedges, clichés, bureaucratic phrasing, nominalisations where a verb would do.
- **Write for the reader's benefit.** Every paragraph answers "why should this person care?"
- **Concrete over abstract.** Name the thing, the place, the number, the person.
- **Depth over decoration.** A concrete detail earns its place only when it says why it matters to the reader. Scenery is not character; insight is. (Yana, 27 Oct)
- **Honest.** No exaggeration, no manipulation, no promises we cannot keep.
- **Short and clear.** One idea per sentence; active voice; simple words.
- **Respect the reader.** No pressure, no begging, no flattery, no conditional calls to action.

Before any draft is shown: read it line by line and ask of each sentence, "what does this mean, and what would the reader lose without it?"

## How to use

1. Read this file and the latest entries in `editorial-log.md`.
2. Decide the page: Sari (personal) or Ranger (company). Never write a post that could belong to both.
3. Copy first, two versions that differ in substance. Creative only after the copy is agreed.
4. Run the checklist. Show Sari one decision, not a menu.
5. Log every change Yana or Sari makes, with who, what, toward what, and the rule it produces.

Rules are Standing (applied twice or stated as a principle) or Situational (one post or event). A situational call becomes standing only when it repeats. Sari, 26 Oct: "some of them are situational… I'll answer it 3 different ways in 3 different days."

## Strategy

- One goal for every post: make the right people want to speak with us. (Sari, 28 Sep)
- The system converts, not the single post. Reach comes from engagement and tags; content gives alignment. (Sari, 19 Oct)
- Weekly minimum, about 50 posts a year. One celebratory to two or three non-celebratory. (Sari, 19 Oct)
- Before an event: proof that we are a winning, mature team with real clients, then technical grounding. (Sari, 19 Oct; Yana, 27 Oct)
- The Ranger page serves North America and the Gulf. It must not look EMEA-only. "US based movement is good for our customers." (Sari, 27 Oct)

## Audience and perception

- Readers: C-suite in industrial and oil and gas, mostly 50+, who do not like to read; plus user-level experts who must see we know their work. Not tech or VC readers.
- They follow factual, technical, market-level voices.
- Be seen as: the innovation team and the trusted experts who bring AI to a whole organization and make it work. Visionary, established, senior, never small. (Yana, 27 Oct; Sari, 7 and 20 Oct)

## Voice constants (both pages)

We are: warm, elegant, simple, confident, the way powerful people speak. Serious and experienced, with energy that is earned and mature.

Reference brand: Adobe. Serious software, easy to approach. "We're easy guys, but we're serious in what we do." (Yana, 27 Oct)

Write as the editor of a premium brand would: protect the image, the perception and the style in every line. Fewer words, each one deliberate.

- A past event needs a reason to be posted now, given in the first lines (a look back, what it confirmed, why it matters today).
- Regional posts stay in their region: a North America post does not mention the Gulf, and the reverse.

We are not: North American startup excitement, hype, lifestyle tone, salesy, begging, clickbait, AI-sounding.

- Energy comes from verbs and specifics, never from exclamation marks or adjectives.
- Say the vision once, plainly, as people who have done it.
- Never ask for the reader's time ("give us 30 minutes"), never ask prospects to share live bid details. (Yana, 27 Oct)
- No listicle hooks or buyer checklists ("three questions that separate…"). (Yana, 26 Oct)
- Claims: every figure sourced; figures from calls stay internal until the client clears them. No client names without permission: signal proof as "clients we already work with" or "companies you would know".
- Form: sentence case; no em dashes, emoji, rule of three, "not X but Y", slogan endings, superlatives.
- Terms: "Techno-Commercial AI" (working term), "inquiry to cash". Never "revenue engine", "seamless", "unlock", "game-changing".

## Footprint

Ranger works in North America, Europe, the GCC and Asia (intro deck: United States, Canada, Europe, GCC, East Asia). Never shrink it to one or two regions. (Yana, 27 Oct)

## Posts are not pitches

Lead with something the reader wants to know (an insight, a question the market is asking, what we see across clients), then who we are as the source of that view, then the event or the people. A paragraph that only lists who we work with and what we do is an advertisement and does not get read. (Yana, 27 Oct)

## Invitations

No conditional calls to action ("If that is a conversation for your team…": of course it is) and no directive "message us here". If a closing line adds no information, end without one. "Comparing notes" and "we look forward to seeing you" mean nothing. (Yana, 27 Oct)

## Positioning: use Sari's own words first

Before writing any line about what Ranger does, reuse these. Do not paraphrase into weaker generic phrases ("bringing AI into…", "AI-powered workflows").

- "Ranger combines industrial expertise and AI to connect the technical and commercial work behind complex industrial projects." (Sari, Slack, 19 Oct: the one-liner for profiles and bios)
- "from inquiry all the way to cash" (Sari's edit, 2 Oct); "Remittance is a very boring word" (Positioning call, 1 Oct)
- Value first: use the intro deck's "Value scoped and measured against your business goals." Do not write "accountable for the value it delivers" in public copy: it reads as AI and sounds like a complaints line. (Yana, 27 Oct, overriding the wording of Sari's 7 Oct edit, keeping its intent)
- "helping industrial teams put AI to work across complex engineering and commercial workflows" (Sari's WETEX edit, 23 Oct)
- "We work with leading EPCs, NOCs and OEMs across the GCC, Europe and North America" (Sari's approved register line)
- Intro deck (Ranger Intro Lean, 4 pages, 21 Oct): "Your partner for techno-commercial AI." "Ranger connects the work, accelerates decisions and aggregates the value." "One partner, accountable for platform, process and results." "Begin with one workflow. Extend the connected capability as value is proven." "Each bid and project informs the next." Stages: Capture, Engineer, Bid, Deliver.
- "I'm not starting with building a knowledge base, I'm starting with giving you agentic value." (Sari, 15 Oct)
- Internal only, never public: "not an AI company, the way Nike is not a shoe company"; "bankable AI value"; "effectively the leading techno-commercial platform for pumps".

## Images

- Real photos first, from Drive or Slack. Check the year on the backdrop.
- Grades never touch skin: calm the background, keep faces natural and present. Heavy filters make people look lifeless. (Yana, 27 Oct)
- Client logos or staff in a photo need client approval.

## Hashtags and tags

- Sari: topic, event, #RangerAI, #ranger_in_the_field.
- Ranger: event, #RangerAI, #RangerInTheField.
- Tag people and their companies at the end; exact LinkedIn page names. A thank-you line with tags is optional when no list is confirmed.

## Checklist

- Recognisably Sari or recognisably Ranger?
- Could another AI vendor paste its name in? Rewrite if yes.
- Would a 55-year-old CEO read the first line without feeling sold to?
- Does it make the right people want to speak with us?
- Every claim sourced, every name cleared, region right for the audience?
- Image senior, natural, current?

## Timing

- Weekdays only. No weekend posts. (Yana, 27 Oct)
- Times are always given in Pacific and Gulf. Never Spain time. (Yana, 27 Oct)
- Default for Gulf audiences: 09:00 Gulf. For North America: early morning US (06:00 Pacific = 17:00 Gulf while the US is on daylight time).
